# Fictional AML Demo Policy

This document is fictional training material for the local AML investigation
prototype. It is not legal advice, a regulatory standard, or an operational
financial-crime policy. Policy references returned by the demo retriever must
be checked by a qualified reviewer before any real use.

## Transaction review thresholds

Transactions above the configured review threshold should be sent for human
review. The threshold is an example workflow setting, not a legal reporting
threshold. Investigators should consider transaction purpose, customer
profile, expected activity, and supporting source evidence before deciding.

## Customer due diligence

Customer due diligence information should be current and consistent with the
customer's expected activity. An incomplete, stale, or inconsistent profile
is a reason to request additional evidence or route the alert to a reviewer.
A customer risk rating is a prioritization signal and does not establish
that a transaction is suspicious.

## Screening matches

A potential politically exposed person or sanctions screening match must be
verified against reliable identifying information. A potential match is not
proof of identity or wrongdoing. Record the screening source and the reason
for confirming or clearing the match.

## Evidence and investigation records

Investigation findings should identify the source file, relevant transaction
or customer fields, and the reviewer decision. Separate observed facts from
analyst interpretation. Preserve enough context for another reviewer to
reproduce the reasoning.

## Human review and outcomes

Automated scores and rules support triage; they do not make a final legal
determination. A trained human reviewer should approve, reject, or escalate
the case and document the rationale. Unresolved evidence gaps should remain
visible in the case record.
